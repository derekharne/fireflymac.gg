Firefly Cloud

Your games, shared.

Firefly Cloud makes it effortless to share full games with coaches, duos, and communities, show off your best clips and montages, and with select plans keep every recording online instead of eating your Mac's storage.

Share full games

Send a whole game with a single link

Share a full match with your 5s team, your duo, a coach, or your community — they watch it right in the browser, timeline and all, with nothing to install and no account to make. Made for quick and easy game reviews from your POV. See a live sample →

Show off the highlights

Your best clips and montages, one link away

Turn your great plays into clips or montages in the app and instantly get a share link. Outplays, pentakills, moments you need feedback on — share them all in a click.

Stay in control

Manage your uploads without leaving the app

Everything you've sent to the cloud lives in the Cloud tab. Quickly grab share links or download games back to your Mac. Everything is managed straight from the app, you'll never have to login to our website to manage your account or uploads.

Firefly Cloud as your primary storage

Spare your Mac's SSD and use us as your main storage

League games eat disk fast. A Primary Storage plan hands that weight to us. Firefly stores your recordings in the cloud automatically but treats them exactly like local files, so you browse, review, and clip right in the app like nothing changed. Upload or download a game at any time or easily keep a copy in both places. Firefly Cloud as primary storage is an optional feature available on select plans.
