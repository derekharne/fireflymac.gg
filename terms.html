Firefly Cloud Terms of Service.

Last updated: July 2026

These are the terms for Firefly Cloud — the optional service that lets you upload recordings from the Firefly Mac app and share them via a public link. They cover how the account works, what you can and can't upload, and how the paid plans operate. Firefly Cloud is a convenience service for game replays — keep a local copy of anything you can't afford to lose; the Mac app stores recordings on your computer by default. Using the Mac app on its own (without signing in to Cloud) doesn't require agreeing to these terms; only the Privacy Policy applies in that case.

What Firefly Cloud is

Firefly Cloud is the service that lets you upload selected recordings, clips, and montages from the Firefly Mac app to storage we manage, and share them with anyone via a public link rendered on app.fireflymac.gg. Sign-in is via Apple. Free, trial, and paid tiers may be offered; the per-tier limits — slots, retention, and whether clips and montages can be uploaded — are displayed in the Mac app's Cloud tab and on this website.

The Mac app itself is a separate piece of software that runs entirely on your computer and doesn't require Cloud — these terms don't apply to that local usage.

Your account

Firefly Cloud accounts are tied to your Apple ID via Sign in with Apple. You're responsible for keeping your Apple ID secure; we have no separate password to manage.

You can sign out anytime from the Mac app, and you can permanently delete your account yourself — immediately — from Settings → Cloud → Delete Account & All Data in the Mac app, which removes your account and all associated Cloud data. If you'd rather not use the app, email support@fireflymac.gg from the address Apple shares with us and we'll delete everything within 7 days of a confirmed request. Backup snapshots, if any, may persist on a separate rotation that purges naturally over time; they are never used to restore data outside of disaster recovery.

Accounts are personal and non-transferable. You may not share your Sign in with Apple credentials, your account, or your Cloud uploads' authenticated API access with anyone else. Each natural person gets one Firefly Cloud account; sharing one account across multiple users or installations is grounds for suspension or termination.

Your recordings and backups

Your Cloud uploads are stored in Cloudflare R2 — object storage engineered for high durability and redundancy across multiple facilities. We make reasonable efforts to keep your uploads safe and available. As with any online service we can't offer an absolute guarantee, but loss of a stored upload should be a rare, exceptional event.

By default the Firefly Mac app also keeps recordings on your own computer, and you can download any Cloud upload back to your Mac at any time from the Cloud tab — so you always have the option to keep your own copy.

The "Lock" badge on a Cloud upload prevents it from being recycled by the automatic quota-replacement system when your slots fill up. It's a slot-management convenience, not a separate backup.
